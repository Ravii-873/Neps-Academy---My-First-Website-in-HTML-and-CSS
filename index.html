Alter do Chão (PA)

Dubbed the "Amazonian Caribbean," this village near Santarém, Pará, sits along the Tapajós River. During the dry season (August to January), receding waters reveal vast white-sand river beaches, notably Ilha do Amor. The area preserves a strong Borari Indigenous identity, celebrated annually during the Sairé Festival. Popular activities include boat trips through the Tapajós National Forest, swimming in warm, clear waters, and tasting regional Amazonian fish dishes.
