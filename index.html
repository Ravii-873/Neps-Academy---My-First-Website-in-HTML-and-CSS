Brasilian Voyagers

Brasilian Voyagers © is an online platform where you can find your next destination in Brazil. Rather than offering traditional cities, touristic spots, a few waterfalls and annual festivals, we choose to show the other side of Brazil, Brasil. With destinations in Nordeste, Sul, Norte and Centro-oeste, you escape from the obvious, lead yourself to the unknown and underrated beauties of this land.

But don't get mad, we also offer the best travel packages to wherever place you wish to visit here! : )

Amazon River viewed from the International Space Station - ISS, 400km above the ground.

We're founded in 2019, after discovering places we never thought existed. This includes country side China, western Europe, southern Argentina, and not less important, Brasil.

Nowadays, Brasilian Voyagers includes travel packages, historic and geographical guides, informative content in all of our posts and, the newest one, a community where you can post your photos, tag and share them, all revised and commented by our specialized team.
